## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Scope

SIA Layer 1 defines the high-level policy orchestration, regulatory compliance mapping, and non-authorial governance protocol for the Sovereign Intent Architecture (SIA). 

In enterprise environments, direct coupling of probabilistic Large Language Models (LLMs) to centralized legacy databases introduces severe structural vulnerabilities, including unconstrained data exposure, hallucination-driven operational drift, and regulatory non-compliance under the EU AI Act and ISO 42001 standards.

SIA Layer 1 serves as an abstract, non-intrusive logic topology operating asynchronously above legacy infrastructure. It translates enterprise governance mandates and European Digital Sovereignty requirements into deterministic boundary constraints prior to execution by local models or deterministic state machines.

---

## 2. Non-Authorial AI Governance Doctrine

SIA is built upon a strict, unyielding non-authorial governance doctrine designed to eliminate algorithmic hallucination risk and satisfy strict auditability requirements:

* **Human System Design Primacy:** Generative AI components, foundation models, and quantized Small Language Models (SLMs) within SIA are deployed strictly as **non-authorial linguistic synthesis assistants**. They possess no authority to design system topology, define state machines, or determine authorization boundaries.
* **100% Human Architectural Origin:** All core data structures, state transition tables, FSM circuit breakers, and 3-Tag schema abstractions (`[Entity]`, `[Factoid]`, `[State]`) originate exclusively from human systems engineering.
* **Deterministic Policy Enforcement:** Probabilistic reasoning is never permitted to make final authorization or state-change decisions. Every output generated by an LLM or SLM is treated as a unverified candidate intent, which must pass through a human-designed, deterministic Finite State Machine (FSM) cage before reaching downstream legacy execution layers.

### Protocol Pipeline Mechanics:
1. **Policy Ingestion:** High-level enterprise policies, compliance mandates, and operational guidelines are parsed by the Layer 1 Policy Engine during offline configuration.
2. **Constraint Formulation:** Policies are compiled into explicit, non-authorial JSON schema constraints defining permitted state transitions, maximum execution hop limits, and mandatory entity isolation tags.
3. **Intent Boundary Distribution:** The compiled constraint schema is injected into the local runtime environment (Layer 2) as an immutable execution boundary, guaranteeing that runtime models operate within pre-defined safe harbors.

## 5. Auditability & Cryptographic Verification

Layer 1 mandates that every transaction processed through the SIA topology yields an immutable, machine-readable audit record.

* **Decision Packet Architecture:** Instead of returning unstructured natural language responses to legacy applications, SIA produces standardized **Decision Packets**.
* **Payload Structure:** A Decision Packet comprises:
  1. `Transaction_ID`: Cryptographic hash of the operational request.
  2. `State_Vector`: Current deterministic state of the FSM runtime.
  3. `Compliance_Hash`: SHA-256 hash validating that the execution stayed within Layer 1 policy boundaries.
  4. `Execution_Status`: Success flag or explicit error code (e.g., `SECURITY_VIOLATION_0x88`).

> **Zero-Trace Assurance:** Decision Packets contain strictly zero raw text payloads. Operational data is retained purely as transient state vectors during edge processing and is subject to immediate C/Rust memory zeroization (`memset_s`).
